How It Works

From browse to drive in four steps.

  1. 01

    Browse & enquire

    Filter by brand, budget, body type, year, or km. WhatsApp us for a quick walkaround video of any car on the floor.

  2. 02

    Visit & inspect

    Step into our South Delhi showroom, see the cars in person, and bring a family member or mechanic if you like.

  3. 03

    Negotiate & pay

    Independent inspection welcome. We lock the price and accept bank transfer, DD, or in-house EMI.

  4. 04

    Drive home

    We complete the RC transfer, deliver the car to your doorstep in Delhi NCR, and hand over a folder with every receipt.
